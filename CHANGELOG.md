# Collection Updates

## November 2026

We introduce the companion collection for *Reconstructing Complete 3D Humans from Limited Observations: A Survey*.

- Literature organized around four observation axes and six compensatory method families.
- Method and dataset inventories, paper sources, and implementation links.
- Searchable catalogue with BibTeX access.
- Reading routes, evaluation guidance, research directions, and reusable tools.

We welcome corrections and additions through issues and pull requests.

# Reading Routes
[Back to repository](../README.md)

Use these as starting points, not ranked recommendations. Names link to complete records through the [paper index](papers.md) and searchable [catalogue](../homepage/index.html).

| Available evidence | Start with | Then examine | Key question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Single full-body image | PIFu, PIFuHD, ICON | ECON, SIFU, TeCH, Human-LRM | Which detail is observed and which is completed? |
| Two or a few views | StereoPIFu, KeypointNeRF | GPS-Gaussian and related feature-aligned systems | Are cameras calibrated and subjects synchronized? |
| Monocular video | Neural Body, HumanNeRF | Vid2Avatar, GaussianAvatar, GauHuman | Does motion reveal previously hidden surfaces? |
| Persistent occlusion | OccNeRF, Wild2Avatar | OccFusion, SOAR, WonderHuman | What anchors appearance where no pixels are observed? |
| Blurred footage | BAGA and Deblur-Avatar | Neighboring restoration pipelines | Is blur modeled jointly or removed before reconstruction? |
| Personal photo collection | PuzzleAvatar | UP2You and album-fusion directions | What is shared across outfits, dates, and poses? |
| Head or hand input | DECA, HaMeR | Portrait4D, Avat3r | Which mechanisms transfer beyond their specialized domain? |
| No subject observation | Conditional generation inventory | Prior interfaces and composition | Is the objective generation rather than subject recovery? |

## Foundations

Read the manuscript preliminaries alongside body-model, implicit-field, NeRF, Gaussian-splatting, and diffusion references in [papers.md](papers.md). Follow the actual paper or official implementation for equations and training details.

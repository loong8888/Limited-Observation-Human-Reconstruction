# Open Problems
[Back to repository](../README.md)

These are research directions discussed by the survey, not established evaluation standards.

| Direction | Missing capability | A useful experimental question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Multi-hypothesis reconstruction | Calibrated uncertainty under missing evidence | Does probability mass cover plausible hidden geometry? |
| Content provenance | Distinguishing measured, propagated, and generated content | Can an evidence map predict where identity fidelity is unreliable? |
| Personal photo albums | Fusion across outfits, years, devices, and poses | Which factors should be shared and which should vary? |
| Degradation-aware inference | Joint handling of blur, damage, and missing content | Does completion preserve identity rather than replace it with a clean generic person? |
| Physical and scene plausibility | Contact, collision, and dynamics constraints | Do plausible renders also produce plausible interactions? |
| Video / 4D priors | Persistent cross-view and temporal identity | Can generated evidence remain coherent under unseen motion? |
| Efficient inference | Balancing shared prediction and per-instance correction | Where does a short refinement step actually improve fidelity? |
| Language models and agents | Reliable orchestration and semantic constraints | Can semantic reasoning help without inventing unsupported evidence? |

## Responsible Use

Human likenesses and personal photographs require consent and careful handling. Do not upload identifiable data, private photo albums, or restricted scans through issues. Respect dataset, model, and commercial-use licenses separately. Generated completion can invent personal attributes and should not be treated as forensic evidence.

# Figures and Icons

Our survey overview is available as `survey-overview.pdf` and as a PNG preview for the repository and catalogue.

The overview illustrates our method taxonomy, composition patterns, and connections to datasets, evaluation, and research directions. Please cite our survey when referring to this framework.

The six small navigation SVGs are Lucide icons sourced via ML Paper Icons. Their original ISC notices are in ICON_LICENSE.txt; they are excluded from the original-documentation license.
